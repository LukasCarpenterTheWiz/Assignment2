About Us

Mrs. Pac's Pizza Barn is a family-owned pizza place with a retro arcade twist. We believe great pizza and a good time belong together.

Our Story

It started with one oven and one very hungry family. Mrs. Pac spent years perfecting her dough recipe at home and feeding anyone who stopped by.

In 1980 she turned an old red barn on the edge of town into a pizza shop, set up a few arcade machines in the corner, and opened the doors. Word spread fast. Today the Barn is where families come for Friday pizza night, teams celebrate big wins, and kids chase high scores while they wait for their pie.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: serve fresh, made-from-scratch pizza at fair prices, and give our neighbors a fun, welcoming place to gather. Every pizza we make should be one we'd be proud to serve our own family.

Meet the Team

Say hi to the crew! You'll find these friendly faces behind the counter, in the kitchen, and out on delivery.

Mrs. Pac - Owner and Manager

Mrs. Pac runs the Barn and still tosses the first batch of dough every morning. Her go-to order is a Classic Pac, extra crispy. You can spot her by the red bow.

The Ghost Crew

Four hard workers who never seem to slow down:

  • Red - Delivery driver. Fastest in town.
  • Blue - Head cook. Runs the oven and the pizza peel.
  • Orange - Carryout counter. Your box is always ready.
  • Pink - Front of house. Drinks, smiles, and free refills.

In the Community

The Barn gives back. We sponsor local youth baseball and soccer teams, host fundraiser nights where 20% of sales go to neighborhood schools, and donate pizzas to the local food pantry every month.
